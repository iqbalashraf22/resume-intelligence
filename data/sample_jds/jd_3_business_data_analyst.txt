Business Data Analyst

Responsibilities

- Analyse customer funnels and find drop-offs

Requirements

- Strong SQL
- Python for data analysis
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI
- Knowledge of A/B testing and basic statistics
- Good communication with non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have
- Google Analytics
- Degree in Statistics or a related field
